# Upwork Proposal Templates

## Short Template (4-6 lines)
Hi [Name], I can set up a reproducible RL trading experiment using SB3 + Hydra + MLflow and deliver tracked metrics + a short report. I can align the reward to your constraints (risk, costs, drawdown). This is research-grade only, no profitability claims. If you share dataset format and target metrics, I can start immediately.

## Medium Template
Hi [Name],
I build research-grade RL trading experiments with Gym environments, SB3 (PPO/A2C/DQN/SAC), Hydra configs, and MLflow tracking. I can integrate your dataset, implement a reward aligned to your constraints (risk, costs, drawdown), and deliver a clean experiment run plus an evaluation report (Sharpe, drawdown, win rate, returns). This is research-only, no profit promises or live unattended trading. If you share your dataset format and success criteria, I can propose a concrete plan and timeline.

## Quick Questions to Send
- What is the dataset format and sample size?
- What assets and timeframe are you targeting?
- Which reward or risk metric matters most (returns, sharpe, sortino, pnl, drawdown)?
- Any cost model or constraints (fees, slippage, position limits)?
- What is the minimum deliverable you need (single run vs multi-run comparison)?
